# Product Overview

Comprehensive overview of the HatoFit mobile application for stakeholders and product managers.

**Audience**: Executives, Investors, Product Managers  
**Last Updated**: February 2026

---

## What is HatoFit?

HatoFit is a cross-platform mobile fitness tracking application built with Flutter that helps users:

- **Track workouts** with guided exercise routines
- **Monitor heart rate** in real-time via Bluetooth devices
- **View progress** through detailed analytics and reports
- **Connect with fitness partners** and gyms

---

## Value Proposition

| For Users | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Convenience** | All fitness tracking in one app |
| **Accuracy** | Real heart rate monitoring from Polar devices |
| **Guidance** | Professional workout videos and instructions |
| **Motivation** | Progress tracking and achievement insights |

## Target Audience

### Primary Users

- Fitness enthusiasts aged 18-45
- Users of wearable heart rate monitors
- People following guided workout programs

### Secondary Users

- Gym partners and fitness companies
- Healthcare providers (wellness programs)
- Corporate wellness programs

---

## Market Position

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Polar Integration**: Native support for Polar heart rate monitors
2. **Multi-Platform**: Single codebase for iOS and Android
3. **Localization**: Support for English and Bahasa Indonesia
4. **Offline Mode**: Works without internet connection

### Market Opportunities

- Growing fitness app market in Southeast Asia
- Increasing adoption of wearable devices
- Rising demand for home workout solutions

---

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

1. **Premium Subscriptions**: Advanced features and content
2. **Partnerships**: Fitness company integrations
3. **Corporate Wellness**: B2B enterprise solutions
