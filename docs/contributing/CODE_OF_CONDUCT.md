# Code of Conduct

Community guidelines for all HatoFit contributors.

**Audience**: All Contributors  
**Last Updated**: February 2026

---

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- **Welcoming**: Be inclusive and friendly
- **Respectful**: Disagree without attacking
- **Helpful**: Support other contributors
- **Constructive**: Give and receive feedback well

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing private information
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Other unprofessional conduct

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

### Consequences

| Behavior | Action |
|----------|--------|
| First violation | Warning |
| Second violation | Temporary ban |
| Severe violation | Permanent ban |

### Reporting

Report violations to: conduct@hatofit.com

All reports will be reviewed confidentially.
