# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using HatoFit.

**Audience**: All Users  
**Last Updated**: February 2026

---

## Account & Authentication

### Q: How do I reset my password?

**A:**
1. On login screen, tap "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email for reset link
4. Create new password

### Q: Can I use multiple sign-in methods?

**A:** Yes. Link Google account in Settings → Linked Accounts.

### Q: How do I delete my account?

**A:**
1. Settings → Account → Delete Account
2. Confirm deletion
3. Data will be removed within 30 days

---

## Workouts

### Q: How do I start a workout?

**A:**
1. Go to Workouts tab
2. Browse or search exercises
3. Tap exercise to view details
4. Tap "Start"

### Q: Can I workout offline?

**A:** Yes. Core workout tracking works offline. Videos require internet.

### Q: How are calories calculated?

**A:** Calories are calculated using:
- Exercise MET values
- Your body metrics (height, weight, age)
- Heart rate data (when available)

### Q: Can I create custom workouts?

**A:** Custom workouts coming in v1.2.0.

## Heart Rate Monitoring

### Q: Which devices are supported?

**A:**
- Polar H10, H9, OH1, Verity Sense
- Any BLE heart rate monitor

### Q: Why isn't my heart rate showing?

**A:** Check:
1. Device is paired correctly
2. Device is on your body
3. Electrodes are moist (H10/H9)
4. Bluetooth is enabled

### Q: How do I pair my Polar device?

**A:**
1. Enable Bluetooth on phone
2. Open HatoFit → Devices tab
3. Tap "+ Add Device"
4. Select your Polar device
5. Follow pairing instructions

### Q: What's the range for Bluetooth?

**A:** Typically 10 meters (33 feet). Walls may reduce range.

## Data & Privacy

### Q: Is my data secure?

**A:** Yes. We use:
- Encrypted data storage
- Secure authentication
- No selling of personal data

### Q: Can I export my data?

**A:** Yes. Settings → Data → Export.

### Q: Where is my data stored?

**A:** Data is stored on:
- Your device (local)
- Firebase cloud (encrypted)
- Your choice for sync

### Q: Who can see my workouts?

**A:** Only you by default. Optional sharing available.

## Technical Issues

### Q: App is crashing

**A:** Try these steps:
1. Force close and restart
2. Clear app cache
3. Reinstall app
4. Update to latest version

### Q: Videos not loading

**A:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Try lower video quality
3. Restart app
4. Clear cache

### Q: Bluetooth not working

**A:**
1. Enable Bluetooth in phone settings
2. Restart Bluetooth on phone
3. Unpair and re-pair device
4. Check device battery

### Q: Battery drain

**A:** Normal during workouts with HR monitoring. Tips:
- Close background apps
- Reduce screen brightness
- Enable battery saver mode
